In short Customer Portal is a read-only companion to Debt Ledger. It lets you view the debt and payment records that a business has authorised you to see. It does not let you create or edit debts, change balances, or record payments. Your connection is limited to your own linked customer account.

2. What Customer Portal does

Customer Portal is a companion app for businesses that use Debt Ledger. A business can invite a customer to connect to a particular debtor/customer record. After you sign in and redeem a valid invitation, the App can show the records that belong to that connection.

  • You may connect to more than one business and view each business account separately.
  • You may view your available balance, debt records, item details, payment history, account activity and supported summaries.
  • You may receive an alert when a connected business records a new debt or a payment for your account.
  • You may create a customer-facing invoice, payment receipt or account statement PDF from information already available in the App.

Read-only service: Customer Portal does not provide controls for adding, editing or deleting business debts, changing financial balances, or recording or processing payments. If a record is incorrect, contact the business that maintains it. The business controls its Debt Ledger records.

The current Customer Portal app does not include Debt Ledger’s AI receipt-scanning feature, subscription billing or in-app advertising.

6. Connected businesses and your financial information

How a connection is created

A business creates an invitation in Debt Ledger and shares its code or QR code with you. Invitations are designed for one-time use and expire after 30 minutes. The backend stores invitation codes as hashes rather than storing the original code as a reusable plain-text credential. When you redeem an invitation, the service creates matching customer-to-business and business-to-customer connection records.

What the business can make available

The business connection identifies the specific debtor/customer record that belongs to you. The App reads the customer-safe debtor summary and related debt/payment records for that linked record, subject to the active connection, account status and the business’s portal access. The business name is used to identify the account. Business phone or location details may also be shown when those fields are supplied through the authorised connection, including on customer-facing PDFs.

The connection does not give you access to the business’s entire ledger, other customers’ records, private owner-only notes or unrelated business profile information. The business remains responsible for the accuracy and lawful collection of the financial information it records.

Disconnecting or revoking access

You can disconnect a business from your Customer Portal account where that option is available. The business can also revoke your connection from Debt Ledger. When a connection is disconnected or revoked, the backend changes the connection status and the service no longer authorises new reads of that business’s linked records through that connection.

Information that was already displayed, downloaded, exported, screenshotted or shared may remain in your possession or in copies held by recipients. Revoking a connection cannot remotely erase a copy you previously saved or sent.

7. Push notifications and notification history

If notifications are enabled and you grant Android notification permission where required, Customer Portal can register an FCM token for your signed-in account. The token is sent to the backend over an authenticated request and stored in a hashed form associated with your customer profile. The token allows Firebase Cloud Messaging to route an alert to your device.

The backend is designed to send customer alerts for two types of event: a new debt recorded for your linked account and a payment recorded for your linked account. Notifications are sent only when the relevant customer connection and account/access checks remain active. The notification message is designed not to include debt or payment amounts or other detailed financial information.

The App keeps a local notification history on your device, including the notification type, title, message, business/debtor references, received time and read state. The current local store keeps up to 100 push events. This history is not a separate cloud notification inbox that you can access from another device.

You can disable notification permission in Android settings. When you sign out, the App attempts to unregister the current device token from the backend. If you later sign in again or reinstall the App, token registration may occur again while notifications are enabled.

8. Local storage, offline information and PDF documents

Information stored on your device

Customer Portal may keep previously loaded business connection information and customer-safe ledger records in app-managed local storage so the App can display information efficiently and support limited offline use. App preferences, notification history and read/unread states are also stored locally. This information is kept within the App’s Android storage area, subject to Android operating-system behaviour, device backups and your device settings.

Offline information may be out of date. When your device is offline, the App cannot guarantee that a displayed balance or transaction list reflects the latest change made by the business. Check the connection status and refresh when internet access is available before relying on a record as current.

PIN and biometric unlocking

If you set a local PIN, the App encrypts the PIN using Android Keystore-backed encryption. If you enable biometric unlocking, the App uses Android’s biometric authentication facilities to control access. The App does not receive or store your fingerprint or face biometric template; biometric matching is handled by Android and your device’s security hardware/software.

Invoices, receipts and statements

The App generates customer-facing PDF invoices, payment receipts and account statements on your device from financial information already available to the App. The PDF generation code does not make a separate backend request or upload the generated PDF to our server. The PDF is temporarily written to app-private cache storage and can then be opened, saved or shared using Android’s system chooser.

If you choose to save or share a PDF, the copy may be stored outside the App or sent to another person or service, such as an email or messaging provider. That recipient or provider will handle the copy under its own privacy practices. You are responsible for checking the recipient before sharing a document containing financial information.

Important: Signing out, disconnecting a business or deleting your online account does not necessarily remove files, screenshots, exports or other copies that you previously saved or shared. You may need to delete those copies separately from your device or the destination service.

11. Retention, account closure and deletion

We keep information for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide Customer Portal, maintain your authorised connections, protect the Service, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. Different types of information have different retention periods.

  • Account and connection records: Retained while your customer account and relevant connections remain active, or until a valid account-deletion request is completed. A disconnected or revoked connection is no longer authorised for new customer reads.
  • Customer profile and device tokens: The backend stores a customer profile and registered device-token records to support the account and notifications. Device tokens are removed as part of customer-account deletion.
  • Notification delivery records: Backend delivery records are used to prevent duplicate delivery and manage retries. Customer-related delivery records are included in the account cleanup process. Your on-device notification history is stored locally and may remain until it is cleared by the App, Android app-data controls or uninstalling the App.
  • Local cache and exported documents: Cached information and PDF files in the App’s private storage may remain on your device until the App removes them, you clear the App’s storage or you uninstall it. Copies saved, printed, screenshotted or shared elsewhere are outside our ability to delete remotely.
  • Limited operational records: The backend may retain account-generation, deletion-status or security/anti-abuse records needed to prevent unauthorised reactivation, protect the Service and maintain reliable deletion processing. These records are not the customer’s active ledger and are not used to restore customer access.

Request deletion of your Customer Portal account

In the App: open Settings and use the account-deletion option, then complete the identity verification requested by the App. The backend requires recent authentication before accepting an account-deletion request.

By email: write to khadharahmed91@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject “Customer Portal account deletion request”. Tell us that you want to delete your Customer Portal customer account. Do not send your password, authentication token or FCM token.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account. When a valid request is accepted, the customer role is disabled first so that the account cannot continue accessing connected business records. A resumable server-side cleanup then removes the customer’s connection records, customer profile, registered device tokens and related notification-delivery records. The cleanup may take time and may be reported as pending until it is complete.

If no other active role remains on the same Firebase account, the backend also revokes authentication sessions and deletes the Firebase Authentication user. Some limited operational security/deletion records described above may remain. Deletion of your Customer Portal account does not delete the business’s original ledger records; the business remains responsible for its own records and applicable retention obligations.

Local copies: Account deletion cannot guarantee removal of cached data, downloaded PDFs, screenshots, printed documents or copies already shared with another person or service. Clear the App’s local storage and delete exported copies separately where you want them removed.

Signing out is not the same as deleting your account. Disconnecting one business is also different from deleting your entire Customer Portal account.

13. Security

Customer Portal uses security controls that include Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check for backend requests, HTTPS for API communication, account-scoped Firestore rules, server-side checks for customer roles and active business connections, and customer-safe record restrictions. The backend checks the matching customer/business connection and account generations before allowing access to linked financial records. Device PIN protection uses Android Keystore-backed encryption; biometric authentication is handled by Android.

These controls are intended to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, but no app, device, network or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your email and Google account, use a screen lock on your phone, keep Android and the App updated, and avoid sharing PDFs or screenshots with people who should not see them. Contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access to your Customer Portal account.
