What this policy is about Customer Portal is an app for an individual customer to view information that a business has specifically linked to that customer. It is not a business-management or bookkeeping app. The app does not give you tools to create debts, change a business's records or make payments.

2. What Customer Portal does

Customer Portal is designed for people who want to view their customer account information from a business that has invited them to connect. After you sign in and connect using an invitation code or QR code, the app can show the information that belongs to the customer record linked to you.

Depending on the information made available for your connection, you can:

  • view the businesses you have connected to;
  • view your customer account balance and related debt records;
  • view item details and payment records associated with your account;
  • view account activity and available summaries;
  • receive account notifications, such as alerts about a new debt, a recorded payment or an account update;
  • create a PDF copy of an available invoice, payment receipt or account statement; and
  • disconnect a business or request deletion of your Customer Portal account.

Customer Portal is read-only. It does not allow you to create or edit debt records, change amounts or balances, record payments, or process payments. If something shown in the app is incorrect, contact the business that maintains the record and ask it to review it.

You do not need to operate a business to use Customer Portal. It is intended for the customer who has been invited to view their own linked account.

3. Information the app handles

Customer Portal handles only the information needed to create and secure your customer account, show your connected customer information, and provide the app's features.

A. Account information

When you register or sign in, the app may handle your name or display name, email address, Firebase user identifier and sign-in status. If you choose Google sign-in, the sign-in service may provide basic profile details such as your name, email address and profile image, where available. If your account has a phone number available from the sign-in provider, the app may display it in your account profile.

B. Connected account information

When you connect to a business, the app handles the identifier for that connection, the business name, the connection status and the identifier of the customer record linked to you. The app may also receive the connected business's phone number or location/address when that information is provided for the connection. These details help you recognise the account you are viewing and may appear on a PDF you choose to create.

C. Your customer account records

The app may handle the customer-specific information made available through your connection, such as your displayed balance, debt entries, item descriptions, quantities, amounts, dates, due dates, payment entries, payment amounts, payment methods, references and payment allocations. The information shown depends on the records available for your linked customer account.

This is not a collection of every customer's information or every record belonging to a business. The app is intended to retrieve records associated with the customer account connected to you.

D. Notification information

If push notifications are enabled, the app handles an FCM device registration token, notification event information, notification title and message, the related business/customer references, the time received and whether you marked a notification as read. The app stores the notification history locally on your device.

E. App preferences and security settings

The app stores preferences such as your selected theme and notification preferences. If you choose to use the app lock, it stores the PIN in encrypted form using Android Keystore-backed encryption and stores whether biometric unlocking is enabled. The app does not receive or store your fingerprint or face biometric template; Android handles biometric verification on your device.

F. Technical information

When the app connects to its services, technical information required for authentication, app verification, network communication and error diagnosis may be processed. This includes Firebase authentication and App Check tokens used to authorise requests. The app obtains these tokens when needed for a request rather than saving them as permanent profile details.

Information the app does not request for its normal features
Customer Portal does not ask for your bank password, card number or online-banking login. The Android app does not request GPS/location permission. Scanning an invitation QR code is used to read the invitation code; it is not a request to collect your physical location.

4. Where information comes from

  • From you: information you enter when creating an account, signing in, redeeming an invitation, setting preferences or contacting support.
  • From your sign-in provider: account details returned by Firebase Authentication or Google when you choose a supported sign-in method.
  • From the business you connect to: the business provides the customer-specific account records and the business identity details that are made available for your connection. Customer Portal displays the information associated with the customer record linked to you.
  • From your device: the app receives an FCM token for push notifications and stores app preferences, notification history and optional app-lock settings locally.
  • From the services that operate the app: Firebase and Google infrastructure process authentication, app verification, authorised data requests and notification delivery.

5. How information is used

Customer Portal uses the information it handles for these purposes:

  • to create and authenticate your customer account;
  • to verify an invitation and connect your account to the correct business/customer record;
  • to show your connected businesses and the customer-specific records made available to you;
  • to keep each connection associated with the correct signed-in customer;
  • to refresh account information and make previously loaded information available for limited offline viewing;
  • to register your device for notifications and show notification history;
  • to create PDF documents from information already available in the app when you request them;
  • to remember your app preferences and protect the app with optional PIN or biometric unlocking;
  • to maintain the security and reliability of the app, investigate errors and respond to support requests; and
  • to process account disconnection, account deletion and other valid privacy requests.

We handle information where it is needed to provide the features you choose to use, protect the app, meet applicable legal obligations or where consent is required by law. You can control notification permission through Android settings.

6. Connected accounts

A business may give you an invitation code or QR code so that you can connect your Customer Portal account to the customer record it maintains for you. You submit the invitation through the app. The service verifies it and, if it is valid, establishes the connection.

The connection is intended to identify one customer record for you at that business. It allows the app to show information associated with that record; it does not give you access to the business's other customers or its general business-management information.

You may connect to more than one business. The app keeps those connections separate so that you can view the relevant customer account under the correct business.

Disconnecting a business

You can use the available disconnect option to remove a business connection from your Customer Portal account. The business may also end or revoke the connection. After the service confirms that a connection is no longer active, the app should no longer be able to retrieve new information through that connection, and the app removes the associated connection from its active local cache.

Disconnecting does not delete the business's original records. It also cannot remove a PDF, screenshot or other copy that you previously saved or shared.

7. Notifications

Customer Portal uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to receive account notifications on your device. When you are signed in, the app can obtain an FCM registration token and register it with the service so notifications can be routed to your device. The app may refresh or register the token again when required.

Notifications may relate to events such as a new debt entry, a payment recorded by a connected business, a due-date reminder, an overdue reminder or a connection/account update, depending on the notification event and your app preferences.

The app stores received notification details and read/unread status locally on your device. The current local notification history stores up to 100 notification events. It is not a cloud inbox that automatically follows you to another device.

You can deny or turn off notification permission in Android settings. If notifications are disabled, the app may not be able to display push alerts. Turning off notifications does not itself disconnect a business or delete your account.

When you sign out, the app attempts to remove the current device token from the service. Token removal may require an internet connection.

8. Information on your device, offline access and PDF files

Local app storage

Customer Portal stores some information locally so it can remember your preferences, show notification history and make previously loaded connection and customer account information available when the network is temporarily unavailable. Local information may not reflect the latest change made by the business. When offline, check the connection status and refresh once you have internet access before relying on a balance or transaction list as current.

App lock

If you enable a PIN, the app encrypts it using Android Keystore-backed encryption. If you enable biometric unlocking, Android performs the biometric verification. Customer Portal does not receive your fingerprint or face biometric template.

PDF documents

If you choose to create an invoice, payment receipt or account statement, Customer Portal generates the PDF on your device using the customer and business information already loaded in the app. PDF generation itself does not require a separate upload of the document to our server. The app temporarily places the generated file in its private cache and opens Android's sharing or saving options so you can choose what to do with it.

If you save, print or share a PDF, a copy may be placed outside the app or sent to another app, service or person you choose. That recipient or service will handle the copy under its own privacy practices. Check the recipient before sharing a document containing account or payment information.

Signing out, disconnecting a business or deleting your account does not necessarily delete copies you previously saved, printed, screenshotted or shared. You may need to remove those copies separately.

9. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Customer Portal uses service providers only as needed to operate the app and provide the features described in this Policy.

  • Firebase Authentication: supports account registration and sign-in.
  • Firebase App Check: helps verify that requests to the app's backend are coming from an authorised app installation.
  • Cloud Firestore and backend services: support the customer account, business connection and authorised customer-record access required by Customer Portal.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: routes push notifications to your registered device.
  • Apps or services you choose: when you share or save a PDF, the information in that document is provided to the destination you select.

We may also disclose information where required by law or valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate misuse, address security issues or protect the rights and safety of others.

Google's handling of information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

10. Retention and account deletion

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Customer Portal, maintain your account and connections, protect the app, complete deletion requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

  • Account information: kept while your Customer Portal account is active and until the account-deletion process is completed.
  • Connection information: kept while a connection is active and as needed to process disconnection or maintain security records.
  • Device notification tokens: kept while registered for your account and removed through the token-removal or account-deletion process.
  • Notification history and app preferences: stored locally on your device until cleared by the app, by clearing app storage or by uninstalling the app.
  • Cached information and PDFs: may remain in app storage until cleared or removed. Copies exported elsewhere are outside our ability to delete remotely.
  • Deletion and security records: limited records may be retained where needed to complete deletion safely, prevent unauthorised reactivation or protect the service.

How to delete your Customer Portal account

You can request deletion from the account-management area in Customer Portal Settings. The app sends an authenticated deletion request to the service. You may be asked to verify your identity or sign in again before the request is accepted.

After the backend accepts the request, the customer account is marked for deletion and access to connected customer information is disabled. Server-side cleanup then removes customer connection records, the customer profile, registered device tokens and related notification-delivery records. This cleanup is designed to be resumable and may remain pending until the process finishes.

If the same sign-in account has no other active role, the authentication account may also be revoked and deleted after customer cleanup. Limited operational security or deletion-status records may remain where needed for the purposes described above.

Deleting your Customer Portal account does not delete the original business records maintained by the business. It also does not guarantee deletion of PDFs, screenshots, printed copies or other files you previously saved or shared. Remove those copies separately from your device or the service where you shared them.

Need help with deletion?
If you cannot use the in-app deletion option, contact khadharahmed91@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password, authentication token or FCM token.

Signing out is not the same as deleting your account. Disconnecting one business is also different from deleting your Customer Portal account.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose whether to grant notification permission, change app notification preferences, use the optional PIN or biometric app lock, disconnect a business and request account deletion.

Depending on the laws that apply where you live, you may also have the right to ask for access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, or to make a complaint to a data-protection authority.

If you believe a debt, payment or balance displayed in Customer Portal is incorrect, contact the business that maintains that customer record and ask it to correct the underlying entry. Customer Portal displays the information made available for your connection; it does not edit the business's records.

For requests about information handled by Customer Portal, contact us using the details in Section 15. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

12. Security

Customer Portal uses security measures such as authenticated requests, Firebase App Check, encrypted network communication, account and connection checks, access restrictions for customer records, and Android's local security features for optional PIN and biometric unlocking.

These measures are intended to protect information, but no app, device, network or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your phone protected with a screen lock, keep the app and Android updated, protect your sign-in account and be careful when sharing PDFs or screenshots.

13. Children

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action under applicable law.
